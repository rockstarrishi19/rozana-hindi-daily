# RozAna — Play Console release checklist

## Account

- Developer account type: Personal
- Support email: Rockstar.rishi19@gmail.com
- Confirm the account's creation date in Play Console. The closed-test requirement below applies to personal accounts created after 13 November 2023.

## Closed test (if applicable)

1. In Play Console, create a Closed testing track and a release.
2. Upload `releases/RozAna-1.1.0-upload.aab` and save the release.
3. Add at least 12 testers to the testing track and publish the test.
4. Share the opt-in link with the testers. They must opt in and remain opted in for 14 consecutive days.
5. Gather tester feedback, address issues, then use Play Console to apply for production access.

Google can ask how testers were recruited, what feedback they gave, and what changes were made. A closed test does not automatically guarantee production access.

## Before production release

- Add real screenshots captured from the final app on a phone.
- Complete the store listing, content rating, target audience, ads, app access, and Data safety sections.
- Review the privacy policy and make sure every content source is licensed or original.
- Upload the signed Android App Bundle and follow Play Console's Play App Signing flow.
